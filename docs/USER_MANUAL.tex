\section{What is SafeShamba?}
SafeShamba is a secure land purchase platform that uses blockchain technology and multisig custody to protect your transactions. Think of it as a digital escrow service where funds are held securely until all conditions are met.

\section{How It Works}
SafeShamba uses a 4-phase workflow:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Intake \& Discovery} --- You submit your land requirements
\item \textbf{Safety Gate} --- Due diligence and document verification
\item \textbf{Execution \& Fiat Bridge} --- Funds are released through secure channels
\item \textbf{Transfer Registry} --- Title transfer and completion
\end{enumerate}

\section{Getting Started}
\subsection{Prerequisites}
\begin{itemize}
\item A Web3 wallet (MetaMask, WalletConnect, etc.)
\item Testnet ETH (Sepolia)
\item A web browser
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Step 1: Connect Your Wallet}
Open SafeShamba in your browser and click ``Connect wallet'' in the top right. Approve the connection in your wallet. Your address will appear in the pill badge.

\subsection{Step 2: Set Up the Factory}
If deploying fresh, get testnet ETH from \href{https://faucet.sepolia.dev}{faucet.sepolia.dev} and run:
\begin{verbatim}
npx hardhat run scripts/deploy.js --network sepolia
\end{verbatim}
Copy the EscrowFactory address and paste it in the Factory field.

\subsection{Step 3: Load an Escrow}
Paste the escrow contract address and click ``Load escrow''. The system will show the current state and your role.

\section{Using SafeShamba}
\subsection{As a Client}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Select plot} --- Choose the land plot during discovery
\item \textbf{Fund escrow} --- Deposit ETH into multisig custody
\item \textbf{Verify document} --- Confirm due diligence documents
\item \textbf{Proceed with purchase} --- Move to execution phase
\item \textbf{Cancel} --- Cancel before execution (refund available)
\end{itemize}

\subsection{As a Notary}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Upload document} --- Submit document hashes
\item \textbf{Set rates} --- Configure deposit and facilitation fees
\item \textbf{Execute fiat bridge} --- Process currency conversion
\item \textbf{Upload lodgement receipt} --- Submit registry documents
\item \textbf{Complete transfer} --- Finalize the transaction
\end{itemize}

\subsection{As a Multisig Owner}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Submit authorized release} --- Approve fund releases
\item \textbf{Purpose selection} --- Choose DD fee, facilitation, or refund
\end{itemize}

\section{Release Authorization}
SafeShamba uses state-gated releases --- funds can only be released when the workflow allows it:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{DD Fee} --- After funding, to Treasury
\item \textbf{Facilitation Fee} --- After completion, to Treasury
\item \textbf{Refund} --- After cancellation, to Client
\item \textbf{Seller Proceeds} --- After completion, to Seller
\end{itemize}

\section{Pricing}
Attractive pricing designed for clients:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Free} --- Create and manage escrows at no cost
\item \textbf{0.5\% facilitation fee} --- Only on completed transactions
\item \textbf{No monthly fees} --- Pay only when you transact
\item \textbf{Volume discounts} --- Increasing savings as you grow
\item \textbf{Revenue share} --- 20\% of facilitation fees for distributors
\end{itemize}

\section{Security Notes}
\begin{itemize}
\item Never share your private key
\item Always verify contract addresses before interacting
\item Use hardware wallets for large transactions
\item Test on Sepolia before mainnet deployment
\item Report vulnerabilities to \href{mailto:security@safeshamba.com}{security@safeshamba.com}
\end{itemize}
