\section{Introduction}
SafeShamba is a secure land purchase platform that uses blockchain technology and multisig custody to protect your transactions. It functions as a digital escrow service where funds are held securely until all conditions are met.

\section{How It Works}
SafeShamba operates through a four phase workflow:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Intake and Discovery} -- You submit your land requirements
\item \textbf{Safety Gate} -- Due diligence and document verification
\item \textbf{Execution and Fiat Bridge} -- Funds are released through secure channels
\item \textbf{Transfer Registry} -- Title transfer and completion
\end{enumerate}

\section{Getting Started}
\subsection{Prerequisites}
\begin{itemize}
\item A Web3 wallet such as MetaMask or WalletConnect
\item Testnet ETH on Sepolia
\item A modern web browser
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Step 1: Connect Your Wallet}
Open SafeShamba in your browser. Click the Connect wallet button in the top right corner. Approve the connection in your wallet. Your address will appear in the status badge.

\subsection{Step 2: Set Up the Factory}
If you are deploying fresh, obtain testnet ETH from the Sepolia faucet and run the deployment script:
\begin{verbatim}
npx hardhat run scripts/deploy.js --network sepolia
\end{verbatim}
Copy the EscrowFactory address and paste it into the Factory field on the left side of the application.

\subsection{Step 3: Load an Escrow}
Paste the escrow contract address into the Escrow field. Click the Load escrow button. The system will display the current state and your role within the transaction.

\section{Using SafeShamba}
\subsection{Client Actions}
If you are purchasing land, you can perform the following actions:

\begin{itemize}
\item Select plot -- Choose the land plot during the discovery phase
\item Fund escrow -- Deposit ETH into multisig custody
\item Verify document -- Confirm due diligence documents
\item Proceed with purchase -- Move to the execution phase
\item Cancel -- Cancel before execution to enable a refund
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Notary Actions}
If you are acting as the legal notary, you can perform the following actions:

\begin{itemize}
\item Upload document -- Submit document hashes to the blockchain
\item Set rates -- Configure deposit and facilitation fee percentages
\item Execute fiat bridge -- Process currency conversion
\item Upload lodgement receipt -- Submit registry documents
\item Complete transfer -- Finalize the transaction
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Multisig Owner Actions}
If you are part of the multisig group, you can perform the following actions:

\begin{itemize}
\item Submit authorized release -- Approve fund releases
\item Purpose selection -- Choose between DD fee, facilitation, or refund
\end{itemize}

\section{Release Authorization}
SafeShamba uses state gated releases. Funds can only be released when the workflow permits it:

\begin{itemize}
\item DD Fee -- Released after funding, paid to Treasury
\item Facilitation Fee -- Released after completion, paid to Treasury
\item Refund -- Released after cancellation, paid to Client
\item Seller Proceeds -- Released after completion, paid to Seller
\end{itemize}

\section{Pricing}
Our pricing is designed to be accessible and fair:

\begin{itemize}
\item Free tier -- Create and manage escrows at no cost
\item 0.5 percent facilitation fee -- Only applied on completed transactions
\item No monthly fees -- Pay only when you transact
\item Volume discounts -- Increasing savings as your transaction volume grows
\item Revenue share -- 20 percent of facilitation fees for distribution partners
\end{itemize}

\section{Security}
\begin{itemize}
\item Never share your private key with anyone
\item Always verify contract addresses before interacting
\item Use hardware wallets for large transactions
\item Test on Sepolia before deploying to mainnet
\item Report vulnerabilities to \href{mailto:security@safeshamba.com}{security@safeshamba.com}
\end{itemize}
