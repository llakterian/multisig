\section{Overview}
This guide explains how to distribute SafeShamba to your clients.

\section{What You Are Distributing}
\begin{itemize}
\item Smart Contracts -- Audited Solidity contracts including EscrowFactory, NotaryMediatedEscrow, and MultiSigWallet
\item Frontend -- React application with SafeShamba branding
\item Documentation -- User manual, deployment guide, and security audit
\item License -- MIT license with a commercial agreement
\end{itemize}

\section{Distribution Methods}
\subsection{Method 1: Deploy and Share}
You deploy the contracts once, then share the frontend URL with clients. This is the simplest approach and works well for early stage distribution.

\subsection{Method 2: White Label Deployment}
Each client receives their own deployment with custom branding. This is ideal for enterprise clients who want a fully branded experience.

\subsection{Method 3: SaaS Platform}
Host everything yourself on your own infrastructure. Charge a recurring subscription fee for ongoing access and support.

\section{Pricing Models}
Our pricing is designed to be attractive to clients while maintaining healthy margins for distributors:

\begin{itemize}
\item Free tier -- Create and manage escrows at no cost
\item 0.5 percent facilitation fee -- Only applied on completed transactions
\item No monthly fees -- Pay only when you transact
\item Volume discounts -- Increasing savings as transaction volume grows
\item Revenue share -- 20 percent of facilitation fees for distribution partners
\end{itemize}
