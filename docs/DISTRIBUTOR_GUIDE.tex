\section{Overview}
This guide explains how to distribute SafeShamba to your clients.

\section{What You're Distributing}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Smart Contracts} --- Audited Solidity contracts (EscrowFactory, NotaryMediatedEscrow, MultiSigWallet)
\item \textbf{Frontend} --- React application with SafeShamba branding
\item \textbf{Documentation} --- User manual, deployment guide, security audit
\item \textbf{License} --- MIT + Commercial Agreement
\end{itemize}

\section{Distribution Methods}
\subsection{Method 1: Deploy and Share (Recommended)}
You deploy the contracts once, then share the frontend URL with clients.

\subsection{Method 2: White-Label Deployment}
Each client gets their own deployment with custom branding.

\subsection{Method 3: SaaS Platform}
Host everything yourself and charge a subscription.

\section{Pricing Models}
Attractive pricing designed for clients:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Free tier} --- Create and manage escrows at no cost
\item \textbf{0.5\% facilitation fee} --- Only on completed transactions
\item \textbf{No monthly fees} --- Pay only when you transact
\item \textbf{Volume discounts} --- Increasing savings as you grow
\item \textbf{Revenue share} --- 20\% of facilitation fees for distributors
\end{itemize}
