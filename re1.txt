The Sizland Land Acquisition Workflow (Internalized Logic)
​
Phase 1: Intake & Discovery
​User Action: The client enters their budget ($5,000), desired size (2 acres), and purpose (Farming/Speculation).
​Sizland Action: The order is routed to the Sizland Admin Panel. Our team sources options and uploads a "Digital Gallery" (Geo-pins, photos, and videos).
​The Filter: The client reviews the options and selects their preferred plot.
​Phase 2: The Due Diligence "Safety Gate"
​Funding: The client funds the Escrow (either the full $5,000 or the initial $200).
​The First Unlock: $200 is released immediately to the Sizland Notary Address to cover the registry search, fuel, and site visit.
​The "Ground Truth": The Notary conducts the search and physical visit. They upload a Comprehensive DD Report to the client's dashboard.
​Decision Gate:
​Proceed: Client gives the green light to initiate the purchase.
​Pivot: If the DD reveals a "burst," the client selects another plot from the gallery. (First 2 site visits are covered by the initial fee; the 3rd requires a top-up).
​Cancel: If the client exits, the remaining escrow is refunded.
​Phase 3: Execution & The Fiat Bridge
​The Release: Upon the client's "Proceed" command, the purchase funds are released from Escrow to the Sizland Notary Address.
​The Bridge: The Notary converts the crypto to KES (fiat) and pays the seller offline.
​Document Custody: The Notary takes possession of the Original Title, Signed Transfers, and LCB Consents.
​UI Update: The client sees: "Funds Disbursed. Sizland has secured all original documents in our legal vault."
​Phase 4: Transfer Registry & Finalization
​The Registry Wait: The Notary lodges the transfer at the Land Registry.
​UI Transparency: The Notary uploads the Official Lodgement Receipt. The UI displays a progress bar and an Estimated Completion Date for the title issuance.
​Completion: Once the new 99-year leasehold title is issued in the buyer’s name, the Notary uploads a digital scan.
​Closing: The final facilitation fee is released to Sizland. The physical title is shipped to the client’s address in Europe, and a Courier Tracking Number is provided in the UI.
​Key Improvements for the Notary Admin Panel
​To make this work, Cheru will build an Admin Dashboard exclusively for the Sizland team to:
​Log Fiat Payments: Record exactly how much KES was paid to the seller against the crypto released.
​Timeline Management: Update the "Expected Date" for the title to keep the client informed during the 2–3 week registry wait.
​Document Management: A central hub to upload the Search Certificate, Sale Agreement, Lodgement Receipt, and the Final Title scan.
​Summary of the "Sizland Advantage"
​By keeping the seller off the platform, you eliminate the technical barrier for rural landowners. The client only trusts Sizland, and Sizland uses its legal and blockchain rails to guarantee the deal.
