Complete Sizland Land Acquisition Workflow Implementation

Updated 4-Phase Workflow

Phase 1: Intake & Discovery
- Client Input: Budget ($5,000), desired size (2 acres), purpose (Farming/Speculation)
- Sizland Action: Order routed to Admin Panel, team sources options
- Digital Gallery: Geo-pins, photos, and videos uploaded by Sizland team
- Client Selection: Review options and select preferred plot

Phase 2: Due Diligence "Safety Gate"
- Flexible Funding: Full escrow ($5,000) OR initial ($200)
- Immediate Release: $200 goes to Sizland Notary for registry search & site visit
- Ground Truth: Notary conducts search and physical visit, uploads DD Report
- Decision Gates: Proceed, Pivot (up to 2 site visits covered), or Cancel

Phase 3: Execution & Fiat Bridge
- Client "Proceed": Triggers fund release from Escrow to Sizland Notary
- Fiat Bridge: Notary converts crypto to KES and pays seller offline
- Document Custody: Notary secures Original Title, Signed Transfers, LCB Consents
- UI Update: Shows "Funds Disbursed. Sizland secured all documents"

Phase 4: Transfer Registry & Finalization
- Registry Wait: Notary lodges transfer at Land Registry
- UI Transparency: Lodgement Receipt uploaded, progress bar + Estimated Completion Date
- Title Issuance: 99-year leasehold title issued, notary uploads scan
- Closing: Facilitation fee to Sizland, title shipped with courier tracking
